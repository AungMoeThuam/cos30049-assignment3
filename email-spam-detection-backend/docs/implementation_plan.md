## Model Discovery & Feature Alignment

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why Feature Extraction is Required**:
> We inspected your model training repository [cos30049-email-spam-detection](https://github.com/AungMoeThuam/cos30049-email-spam-detection.git) and found that your models are trained on a combined feature space using a **`ColumnTransformer`** (`linear_svm.ipynb`, `logistic_regression.ipynb`, `naive_bayes.ipynb`):
> 
> ```python
> preprocessor = ColumnTransformer([
>     ("text", TfidfVectorizer(max_features=50000, ngram_range=(1,2)), "text"),
>     ("numeric", StandardScaler(), NUM_COLS)
> ])
> ```
> 
> Because of this, the loaded pipeline models **cannot accept raw text strings directly**. They expect a pandas DataFrame containing a `"text"` column and the 9 numeric columns defined in `NUM_COLS` (e.g. `num_urls`, `num_exclamation`, `capital_ratio`, etc.).
> 
> To align with your models and avoid runtime crashes:
> 1. We **must** convert any input (a full email or a single split sentence) into a `pd.Series` and pass it through `extract_features()` from [utils.py](file:///Users/showwaiyan/Dev/cos30049-assignment3/email-spam-detection-backend/app/utils.py).
> 2. The resulting DataFrame (`pd.DataFrame`) is then passed directly to the model pipeline: `model.predict_proba(df)`.
